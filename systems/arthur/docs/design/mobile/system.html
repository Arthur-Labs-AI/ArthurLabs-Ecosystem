Marginalia Mobile · system

Mobile adapts the locked desktop language. It reuses ../exploration/arthur.css for every colour, relation colour, radius, easing and duration, and adds only what a touch screen needs. Nothing below changes a desktop token. Board · Playground

Principle

Desktop = explore your thinking.

Rail, inspector beside the list, a full-bleed graph, a horizontal lineage. Reading and comparing.

Mobile = capture your thinking.

Capture, organize, glance, continue. One column, one thumb, one primary action always in reach.

Navigation: the notebook edge

A five-slot bottom bar. The centre slot is not a floating button. It is an index tab: the ink-filled primary from the desktop rail, turned into a tab that sticks 12px out of the page edge, carrying the accent thread-knot (the logo’s apex) on its top edge. It stays in the bar so it never covers content, and sits in the lowest, most reachable row of the screen.

HiddenDuring recording, in Idea detail (replaced by Revise / Branch), and while the keyboard is up.

Same two families as desktop; sizes step up because the reading distance is shorter. Cyrillic falls through to Source Serif 4 and Inter, both already bundled by the app.

Surfaces

Still hairlines and whitespace. Mobile raises three things: the Home page (the composer), sheets and the toast. Selection is a wash on tap, never a bordered card. Lists are separated by hairlines, not containers.

Relation geometry on a phone

Unchanged from desktop: line shape is the kind, colour only reinforces it. The legend is also the filter (in the Filter sheet), and each relation row in the List carries the same swatch.
