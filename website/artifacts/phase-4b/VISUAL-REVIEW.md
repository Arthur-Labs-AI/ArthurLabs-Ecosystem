## Header

- Composition: brand left, compact central Products / Full Suite / Hardware, contextual Downloads or Explore action right. At 768px and below a native details surface exposes all three products plus ecosystem links; it is not a hover menu.
- Hierarchy and material: graphite body, restrained ivory highlight, reflective top edge, rounded raised frame and controlled shadow. Readability relies on visible material density, with blur as an enhancement. The dropdown is denser to avoid the underlying headline competing with its labels.
- Product identity: purple Nexus diamond, green Sentry monogram and warm Studio initial; names/descriptions are visible and official-route active states are distinct. Original ArthurLabs brand is retained.
- Density and ergonomics: compact outer header; product rows remain generous hit targets. Panel remains within viewport in smoke checks. Native details mobile controls retain their visible plus/minus indicator.
- Layering: header z-index 100 sits above the existing noise overlay (80), cinematic scenes and local Nexus navigation; dropdown remains above SVGs. Header occupies the existing navigation-height contract without changing film geometry.
- Fallback: CSS provides an opaque graphite material before backdrop-filter enhancement. A browser without backdrop-filter was not available for native fallback-engine validation.
