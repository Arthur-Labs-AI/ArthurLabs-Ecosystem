**2026-10-19 · NOT RELEASED · bench prototype only · no human electrode contact**

## A. Executive summary

**Manufacturing verdict: CONDITIONAL — FIXES OR FACTORY CONFIRMATION REQUIRED. Do not place an unconditional PCBA order now.** The current electrical design is a plausible bench prototype, and no new net-connectivity defect was found. It is not a qualified wearable or a fully approved assembly package. Factory handling, silk CAM treatment, component sourcing and placement orientation remain open. The custom XIAO attachment also requires explicit acceptance of an unproven physical assembly process.

**Body-contact verdict: NOT ESTABLISHED; human contact remains BLOCKED.** The board has no galvanic isolation. Turning off its analog regulator does not disconnect USB ground or provide a protective isolation barrier. The USB-connected/body-contact prohibition remains mandatory but is insufficient to establish safety on battery either. Qualified independent review and measured normal/single-fault current evidence are required before any person is connected.

Executed on the existing `codex/revb-horizontal-jst` branch, HEAD `98cc84e07fe4cf2791a56baba2cde3e9bb599724`, including the pre-existing uncommitted ECO. **Nothing in the canonical design, libraries, rules, prior evidence or manufacturing packages was edited. No commit was created.** See [preservation-check.json](preservation-check.json) for the final hash comparison.

Principal results:

- KiCad 10.0.6: **ERC 0 violations; DRC 0 errors, 1 warning, 0 unconnected items, 0 parity issues**. The warning is U1's embedded-versus-board-level keepout representation; matching pad geometry and all eleven equivalent rule areas were confirmed. It remains a recorded warning, not a suppressed pass.
- The existing NOT_RELEASED package is current: all ten Gerbers match a new scratch export after removing timestamp lines; drill data differ only in the dated header. All **192 drill coordinates/diameters** independently match the PCB: 191 × Ø0.30 mm vias plus one Ø1.90 mm plated post hole. BOM has 28 groups/126 placed references; CPL has the same 126 references and correct arithmetic transforms.
- PCB outline remains **51.30 × 64.95 mm**, with 1 mm corner arcs. There is no requirement to fit the superseded 61 × 52 mm PCB target. No fixed enclosure size is assumed.
- An independent Gerber check found **0.09 mm silk-to-pad gaps**, below JLCPCB's published 0.15 mm pad-to-silk distance. Current DRC permits 0 mm silk clearance, so its result does not cover that factory requirement.
- Every BOM line was reviewed. Three declaration errors were found: 10 MΩ `RC0603FR-0710ML` is **1%, not 5%**; `CC0603KRX7R9BB104` is **50 V, not 16 V**; `CL21A226MAQNNNE` belongs to the **25 V**, not 10 V, base code. These are metadata discrepancies, not evidence of underrated parts. Exact sourcing/stock is not approved on any line.
- The BAV199/`BAV199,215` discrepancy is an obsolete exact-string oracle, resolved with manufacturer identity evidence. Conversely, the selected JSCJ Q3001 must not be qualified using another vendor's 2N7002 limits.

This is an independent re-examination of design files and primary specifications, **not a review by a qualified medical electrical safety engineer, physical testing, certification, factory approval, or a complete worst-case circuit simulation**. Remaining limitations are explicit below.

### Full electrical review

**All four differential channels.** Fresh XML confirms Jx01 pin1→Rx01 10k→HIZ_P and pin2→Rx02 10k→HIZ_N. Each HIZ leg has100 pF to GND,10M to VCM and BAV199 pin3 common junction, pin1 GND, pin2 AFE_3V0. U501 handles CH1/CH2 and U502 CH3/CH4 as eight unity followers, not instrumentation gain stages. Each buffered leg drives2.2 uF AC coupling,100k return to GND,1k series into ADC and 1 nF shunt; each pair has10 nF differential. D103/104 equivalents use pin1 GND, pin3 AIN and pin2 intentionallyNC, providing only the lower external clamp. ADC pin ordering correctly alternates P/N by channel, including CH2/CH4 reversed manufacturer pin sequence. All corresponding footprints/net mapping match fresh XML.

Ideal input DC resistance is about10M+10k per leg; at50 Hz the100 pF plus package/trace capacitance reduces magnitude. The secondary layout model estimates9.45 MΩ with~111 pF per node, **not a measured dry-electrode impedance**. Contact mismatch, cable capacitance, sweat/flux contamination and resistor/diode leakage can turn common mode into differential noise. The inherited model estimates~−39.5dB conversion for100 kΩ electrode imbalance at50 Hz; this is not measured amplifier CMRR. Cleaning, guarded probing and real source-impedance tests are necessary.

The unloaded AC corner is1/(2π×100k×2.2 uF)=0.723 Hz. A newly solved differential half-circuit with ADC300 kΩ typical differential resistance (gain1),1k series,1 nF shunt and 10 nF cross capacitor gives a corner near1.2 Hz and~0.993 midband transfer. At high PGA gains the ADC impedance differs, so the exact result changes. The differential RC LP scale is approximately7.6 kHz, not a450 Hz anti-alias cutoff. Digital filtering/ODR selection is essential. Large input/motion offsets can saturate the PGA; for gain16 the nominal differential full scale is±75 mV. No firmware configuration was qualified here. The analog-noise floor estimate of~0.64µVrms over20–500 Hz with zero source resistance excludes ADC, cable, RF,1/f/offset drift and interference; it is not an acceptance certificate.

**Reference/“RLD”.** R5003/R5004 form100k/100k divider from AFE_3V0 with 1 uF+100 nF, feeding U503A via10k. U503A is a unity VCM buffer; its output connects to the eight10M bias returns and through R5002 100k to J5001 pin2/TP5. This is **fixed mid-supply reference drive, not closed-loop sensing/cancellation of body common mode**. J5001 pin1 is intentionallyNC. Unused U503B/C/D are followers biased to VCM; no floating amplifier inputs were found. NominalVCM1.5 V; divider mismatch alone±15 mV at3.0 V, plus rail/opamp errors. Divider RC≈55ms; AC-coupling τ≈220ms unloaded. Cable-driven stability, contact motion and settling remain physical tests.

**ADC and analog limits.** TI PW20 pinout is consistent with the netlist, including CAP=220 nF to GND, separate1 uF AVDD/DVDD decoupling, commonAGND/DGND and8.192 MHz CLKIN. The[ADS131M04 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads131m04.pdf), §§6.1/6.3/6.5/10, permits supply ordering but requires pins within their limits. AIN absolute limits are−1.6 V..AVDD+0.3 V; recommended range at gain≥8 is−1.3 V..AVDD−1.8 V. Startup AC feedthrough from~1.5 V buffers can violate that recommended upper range at3 V even if not necessarily absolute maximum. ADC bias current through100k returns, DC recovery, clamp current and shutdown whereAVDD approaches0 need scope verification. Do not infer that the “negative voltage capable ADC” tolerates arbitrary positive off-rail excitation.

**Power/battery/charger.** J1 pin1=VBAT, pin2=GND. VBAT feeds the module and the analog branch throughR4001 4.7 Ω/C4007 22 uF intoTPS7A2030PDBVR. C4005/C4006 are1 uF decouplers. This LDO has active discharge, pin4NC andEN high≥0.9 V/low≤0.3 V specifications. Its effective output capacitance must stay≥0.47 uF; nominal1 uF alone is not a bias/temperature proof. With~14 mA analog demand, R4001 drops~66 mV; lower battery headroom/dropout, radio battery sag and LDO dissipation must be measured. A 1S cell at4.2 V is within input ratings; reversed polarity or a different battery is not protected by this host. No battery, PCM or charge-temperature sensor has been specified by this audit.

XIAO3V3→FB1→DVDD_F feeds ADC digital/XO continuously while analog can turn off. FB1's primary rating500 mA andDCR0.38 Ω are adequate nominally for this small branch; theLC response/load transients remain unmeasured. Seeed's current “plain” PDF actually contains a Sense-labelled design withSGM40567-4.2XG/TR charger and annotation24000/220k≈110 mA, plus3V3 buck. This supports the architecture but **does not establish the exact delivered plain module revision or its charging behavior**. Use a battery simulator, not a non-sinking bench PSU exposed to charger output, for charger tests.

**USB interlock and GPIO.** Fresh XML: D0/GPIO1=VBAT_SENSE (1M/1M +100 nF), D1/GPIO2=DRDY, D2/GPIO3=SYNC_RESET, D3/GPIO4=AFE_EN_REQ; D4/GPIO5 andD5/GPIO6 intentionallyNC; D6/GPIO43=USB_PRESENT, D7/GPIO44=CS, D8/GPIO7=SCLK, D9/GPIO8=MISO, D10/GPIO9=MOSI. These agree with the Seeed header mapping. Firmware must preserve pinmux, reset behavior and board-specific functions; no deployed firmware or boot waveform was tested.

USB_PRESENT uses100k top/150k bottom:0.6×VBUS nominal,2.7..3.15 V over4.5..5.25 V, with resistor tolerance and MCU rail limits to be included in hardware tests. VBAT_SENSE is0.5×VBAT,2.1 V at4.2 V; source impedance500k with100 nF requires adequate ADC settling and calibration. Q3001 gate uses10k top/100k bottom from VBUS; at4.5 V and±1%,minimum≈4.083 V (legacy model's4.32 V assumes a higherVBUS bound). Q3001 must sink about0.33 mA when MCU drives3.3 V through10k. No exactJSCJ guaranteed on-resistance is published at that minimum gate voltage; bench verification across limits is required. Open MOSFET or gate-path failure defeats inhibition; drain-source short disables AFE. Neither outcome disconnects ground. R3009 4.7k loadsVBUS to counter battery-fed reverse leakage, but the asserted residual voltage depends on actual module diode/temperature, so verify it on the selected module.

**Protection and likely failures.** Input clamps are low leakage switching diodes, with temperature-dependent leakage and forward voltage, not system ESD-qualified protectors. Device HBM ratings do not prove exposed electrode/cable ESD immunity. Internal opamp clamps may conduct before external BAV199 fully clamps; actual injected current/rail pumping must be assessed. Series10k at230Vrms would dissipate5.29 W, orders beyond its0.1 W rating; this is a failure illustration, **not a permissible test or protection claim**. Input/DC overvoltage, wrong harness, reference overload, LDO short, BAT reverse and contamination are not covered by DRC.

### Layout and mechanical assessment

The geometric check found track widths0.15/0.20/0.25/0.30/0.50 mm; Ø0.6/0.3 mm vias have0.15 mm annular rings. Independent class-aware comparisons report no clearance violation, no foreign copper within0.25 mm of holes and no hole-hole gap below0.5 mm. Closest copper to outline0.55 mm, chiefly north JST retention pads. In1 andIn2 each retain one GND fill polygon in the inspected cache. In1 reference-plane sampling covers99.86% of outer signal samples after excluding own via holes; onlyVBAT has uncovered samples near the module/post region. This is a plan-view model, not proof of high-frequency return impedance. SPI transitions onIn2 across the thick core and signal-via ground distances merit measurement (AUD-17).

The input area keeps HIZ/electrode traces on outer copper without vias; native custom rules restrict digital routes fromZONE_A and enforce SPI separation.
