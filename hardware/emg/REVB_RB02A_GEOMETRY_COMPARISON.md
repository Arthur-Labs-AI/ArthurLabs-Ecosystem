# A.R.T.H.U.R EMG Rev B — RB-02A Geometry Comparison (Seeed PCB vs RB-00.6)

Date: 2026-10-10. Companion to [REVB_RB02A_XIAO_V14_CAD_VERIFICATION.md](REVB_RB02A_XIAO_V14_CAD_VERIFICATION.md).
Machine-readable source of every number below: `rev-b-xiao/evidence/rb02a/comparison.csv` / `comparison.json`
(157 rows, regenerated by `rev-b-xiao/tools/run_rb02a_checks.sh`). Nothing here was typed in from a drawing: the CAD side is
computed from the Seeed KiCad PCB; the SPEC side is the RB-00.6 text.

**Source caveat (read first).** The CAD file is Seeed's **XIAO ESP32-S3 *Sense* v1.5** main-board PCB. The URL Seeed labels
"plain v1.4" serves the byte-identical archive. It is used here as the best available manufacturer geometry; it is **not**
proven to be the plain-v1.4 production board (see the verification report §1–§2).

## 1. Frames and the 180° transform (re-derived, not copied)

| Frame | Definition |
|---|---|
| CAD file | KiCad board coordinates, top view, +x right, +y down; module outline bbox X 139.6111…157.3911, Y 94.4361…115.5711 |
| Module (u, v) | u = X − 139.6111 (0 on the D0–D6 edge, 17.78 on the D7–5V edge); v = Y − 94.4361 (0 on the USB-end edge, 21.135 at the far edge) |
| Board (x, y) | module rotated 180° in plane (top face up): x = 72.78 − u, y = 32.00 − v |

Validation of the handedness against independent evidence in the same package: in the CAD top view the U.FL (`ANT1`) is at the
left (u = 4.04) and the B2B connector (`J3`) at the right (u = 11.05) with USB at the top; the Seeed plain-module render
(`Top view.jpg`) shows U.FL bottom-left and B2B bottom-right with USB on top. Hence a 180° **in-plane rotation** (not a mirror)
puts D0–D6 on the east side with D0 nearest the USB end, as RB-00.6 assumes.

The only systematic difference to RB-00.6 is a constant **−0.021…−0.022 mm in y**: RB-00.6 places the USB-end edge at
module-frame −21.02 (the Seeed footprint courtyard) whereas the PCB edge is at −21.041 relative to the same footprint origin.

## 9. Cross-check between two Seeed PCB files

`ESP32S3.kicad_pcb` in `Seeed-Studio/OPL_Kicad_Library` (commit b0035c5, 2026-06-23; a different build with 2 signal layers and
J1/J2 half-hole footprints instead of the `XIAO-14P-Add-On` footprint) has the **identical** outline bbox and a **maximum
pad-centre difference of 0.000 mm** over 42 pads (14 half-holes, TP1–TP8, BAT0, GND0, USB pads/shell). The mechanical geometry
is therefore stable across at least two independent Seeed design files; this supports (but does not prove) that the plain
production board shares it.
