## 1. Decision

```
ONE 4-layer EMG PCB  +  ONE Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 (plain, UNSOLDERED-header version)
                     +  ONE protected 1S LiPo on a JST-PH 2-pin
```

- The XIAO is **hand-soldered by its 14 side castellations** to purpose-designed extended pads.
- **BAT+ reaches the board through a short solid-copper post** soldered to the XIAO BAT+ pad *before* mounting. The post drops into a plated hole and is soldered from the underside.
- **No** hidden-pad reflow, hot plate, second PCB, cable, Sensor Bus connector, or test PCB.

### Stop-condition review

None of the four stop conditions is triggered.

| Stop condition | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| BAT+ needs hidden-pad reflow | **No** | The BAT+ pad is soldered while the XIAO is loose and upside down, so the pad is fully visible. The board-side joint is on the board underside, open to an iron. See the Mounting Spec §3 |
| Castellation hand soldering unrealistic | **No** | 1.50 mm pads, 1.04 mm gaps, 2.54 mm pitch. A 1.30 mm toe is outside the module on both long sides, and nothing within 1.5 mm blocks the iron. See the Mounting Spec §2 |
| Unacceptable RF/noise placement | **No** | Toe copper sits 5.7 mm from Zone B; the ESP32 die is 10.8 mm from Zone B and ~20 mm from Zone A. The U.FL is at the module's far (north-east) corner with the cable exiting east. The burst loop stays at x ≥ 53.7 mm (§5) |
| USB inhibit not fail-safe | **No** | Direct mount has no interconnect. XIAO absent, unpowered or in reset leaves the pull-downs holding EN low. The AND logic sits at the TPS7A20 EN pin (§6) |

## 2. Why this reverses the RB-00.6-discussion "brain carrier" proposal

The separate brain carrier with a JST-GH 15-pin Sensor Bus is **withdrawn from the active Rev B architecture**. It stays documented here as a *rejected alternative*.

- **Cost is now a hard constraint.** The carrier added ≈ $36 to the 2-unit prototype (second PCBA order, GH connectors, cables) and ≈ $3/unit at 100.
- **The assembly constraint it was meant to solve is solved another way.** No fine-pitch or hidden-pad work is required, thanks to the hand-solderable pads and the BAT+ post.
- **Its main safety argument disappears.** A single VBUS contact in a harness could fail unsafe; with direct mounting there is no harness.
- **Spare strategy:** a second complete Rev B device is the competition spare, so XIAO swap-ability is no longer needed.

What is lost:
- the brain-agnostic bus;
- the extra 5–10 cm between the burst loop and the leads.

The loss is partly recovered by the orientation choice in §4.

## 3. Board and zones (board frame: origin at the north-west corner, +x east, +y south, mm)

| Zone | x range | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A — analog AFE | 0 – 39.0 | Electrode JST-PH ×5 on the south edge, input protection, TLV9064 ×2, VCM/U503, AC coupling/AAF |
| B — ADC | 39.0 – 48.0 | U6001 (analog pins west, digital pins east), 8 × BAV199 ADC-input clamps, AVDD/DVDD/CAP decoupling, X1 near pin 17. North part: U4002 TPS7A2030 |
| C — boundary | 48.0 – 53.7 | 33 Ω SPI series R, CS/SYNC pull-ups, DVDD ferrite filter, inhibit cluster (north), 4.7 k VBUS load and D-pin networks (south). **Parts taller than 1.2 mm only at x ≤ 49.7 or in the north strip (y < 12.5)** |
| D — brain | 53.7 – 75.0 | XIAO + hand-solder pads, BAT+ post, J_BAT + VBAT split + R_PRE/C_PRE in the north strip (y 0–11) |

- **Board: 75.0 × 32.0 mm** (estimate; the USB-C receptacle overhangs the south edge by 1.51 mm). **[RB-02] Placed board: 97.0 × 32.0 mm.** Zone A grew by 22 mm because the five JST-PH B2B courtyards (9.4 mm each, 47 mm) plus the ADC-side input network do not fit the 39 mm estimate; Zones B–D and the XIAO are unchanged (REVB_RB02_PLACEMENT_REPORT.md §2).
- This is larger than the RB-00 estimate of 62–68 × 28–30. The growth comes from:
  - the 1.3 mm toes on both sides;
  - the 5.7 mm boundary zone;
  - the north strip for J_BAT;
  - the 8 extra ADC-input clamps.

## 4. XIAO orientation: rotated 180° (USB-C → south edge, east end)

Module outline: x 55.00–72.78, y **10.865**–32.00 **[RB-02, D-1: PCB length 21.135, was 21.02]**. The USB end is flush with the south board edge.

| Criterion | 180° (USB south) | −90° (USB east), the RB-00.5 proposal | 0° (USB north) |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.FL position | **North-east corner, far from Zones A–C.** Cable exits east over the board edge | North-west corner, *facing Zone C* | South, near the electrode edge ✗ |
| SPI pins D8–D10, CS D7 | **West side, facing the ADC** (≈ 8–10 mm runs) | South side, west end | East side, away from the ADC ✗ |
| 3V3 / GND / 5V pins | West side, next to the DVDD filter/inhibit | South side | East ✗ |
| BAT+ pad | East half, mid-length → post at (68.34, 21.86) | North half | West half, near Zone C ✗ |
| USB access | South wall, 21 mm from the nearest electrode connector | East short wall | North wall |
| Hand-solder access | Both long sides open (west: Zone C keep-out band; east: board edge) | Same | Same |

**180° wins on antenna, SPI length and battery loop.** The cost is that the USB-C shares the south wall with the electrode cables, at the opposite (east) end. Electrodes must never be connected while USB is attached, so this is an enclosure-labelling issue, not an electrical one.

Consequences:
- D0–D3 (VBAT_SENSE, DRDY, SYNC, AFE_EN_REQ) land on the east side. They are routed north through a 1.3 mm F.Cu channel between the BAT+ post and the east pads, then west along the north strip.
- These are slow/DC control lines, so the extra length (~30 mm) is acceptable.

## 5. Battery and current-return architecture

```
J_BAT+ (north strip, x≈58–66) ─┬─► B.Cu/In2 VBAT pour (≥1.0 mm) ─► BAT+ post (68.34, 21.86) ─► XIAO BAT+ ─► XIAO charger/buck/radio
       (VBAT split AT the pad) └─► R_PRE 4.7 Ω ─ C_PRE 22 µF ─► U4002 (Zone B/C north) ─► AFE_3V0 ─► Zones A/B
J_BAT− ─► In1 plane ◄─ XIAO castellation pin 13 GND (west side, y 26.56) ◄─ XIAO GND pour ◄─ burst return
```

**BAT− via pin 13 is valid.** Seeed's module netlist puts the bottom BAT− pad (GND0) and castellation 13 on the same `GND` net: the module's B.Cu GND pour.

Rules:
1. VBAT splits **at the J_BAT+ pad** (star). The burst branch and the AFE branch share no copper beyond the pad.
2. The burst loop (J_BAT → post → XIAO → pin 13 → plane → J_BAT−) lies entirely at **x ≥ 53.7** (Zone D).
   - J_BAT sits north-centre of Zone D, so the plane return from pin 13 runs *diagonally under the module* rather than along the Zone C edge.
   - Estimated loop area ≤ 250 mm².
3. Pin 13 connects to In1 through **≥ 3 GND vias placed immediately west of its toe pad** (outside the pad, tented). J_BAT− gets ≥ 3 vias.
4. Zone C ground returns (LDO, DVDD filter, inhibit) use their own vias at x ≤ 51. None share the pin-13 → J_BAT− corridor.
5. **USB return:** VBUS/charging current lives on the XIAO module. On our board only the ≤ 50 mA charge current flows, in the same Zone-D loop. The AFE is inhibited whenever VBUS is present.
6. **AFE return:** ≤ 13 mA from Zones A/B to U4002/C_PRE (north Zone B/C) → J_BAT−. It never crosses the burst corridor.
7. Plane-current estimate: 300 mA over ≈ 4 squares of 0.5 oz In1 ≈ 1.2 mV inside Zone D. This decays over millimetres toward Zone C and never touches the Zone A reference.

## 6. Hardware USB inhibit (unchanged logic; relocated)

```
XIAO pin 14 (5V = VBUS) ─┬─ R_VBUSLD 4.7 kΩ → GND            (AT pin 14, Zone C south)
                         ├─ 100 k/150 k → D6 USB_PRESENT     (optional, sense only)
                         └─ R_VG_S 10 k ─ Q3001 gate ─ R3004 100 k → GND
Q3001 (2N7002) drain ──────────────────────────► AFE_EN = U4002 EN (Zone B/C north)
XIAO D3 ─ channel/north strip ─ R_EN_S 10 k ────► AFE_EN
AFE_EN ─ R_EN_PD 100 k → GND  (AT the EN pin; plus the TPS7A20 internal 500 k)
```

- **Cluster location:** Q3001, R_VG_S, R3004, R_EN_S and R_EN_PD sit within 3 mm of the U4002 EN pin, at the XIAO/TPS7A20 boundary (north Zone C). R_VBUSLD sits at pin 14.
- **Result:** a missing, unpowered, resetting or crashed XIAO leaves EN low. USB present forces EN to ≈ 1 mV. The truth table in the interface spec §4 is unchanged.
- **Why D3 must stay AFE_EN_REQ:** D7 (GPIO44, UART RX) has a weak pull-up at reset, which would enable the AFE through 10 k/100 k. D3 is kept (its power-up glitch is low, the safe direction).

## 7. RF / noise rules (frozen)

1. Wi-Fi OFF during acquisition. The BLE connection interval is fixed and characterised by FFT.
2. Distances:

   | From | To | Distance |
   |---|---|---|
   | XIAO toe copper | Zone B | **5.7 mm** |
   | XIAO body | Zone B | 7.0 mm |
   | ESP32 package | Zone B | ≈ 10.8 mm |
   | XIAO toe copper | Zone A (high-impedance nodes) | ≥ 14.7 mm |

3. Antenna: the U.FL cable exits **east**. The antenna element goes in the enclosure's east/north-east end, ≥ 30 mm from the Zone A connectors. Never route the cable over Zones A–C.
4. SPI crosses only Zone C (west pins → ADC digital side). Default **SCLK = 4 MHz**; 8 MHz supported.
5. Solid In1 GND, one GND net, no split. Local 8.192 MHz XO (SCTF SX3M8.192M20F30TNN, C2901557; RB-01R).
6. **No F.Cu copper under the XIAO's exposed bottom pads 15–22 and 25** (EN, USB D±, JTAG, GND window). Only tented vias inside the designated via field.

## 8. Everything else is unchanged from RB-00/RB-00.5

AFE supply (VBAT → 4.7 Ω → 22 µF → TPS7A2030P), DVDD_F filter, single GND, SPI terminations (33 Ω ×3, 100 Ω DRDY), J5001 pin 1 NC, 8 × BAV199 ADC clamps after the 1 k, GND-referenced 100 k bias, 10 k into U503A +IN, X1, JLC Economic single-side SMT, zero via-in-pad.
