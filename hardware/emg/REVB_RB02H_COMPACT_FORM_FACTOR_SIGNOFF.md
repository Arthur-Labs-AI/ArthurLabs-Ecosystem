### 1.4 Thickness (assumed walls 1.2 mm; battery, PCB and JST values documented)

| Stack | Above PCB | Total (floor 1.2 + battery 5.3 + foam 0.5 + PCB 1.606 + parts + lid 1.2) |
|---|---|---|
| Over the battery pocket, parts ≤ 1.45 mm (0805 22 µF) | 1.45 | ≈ 11.3 mm |

The electrode connectors set the enclosure thickness and are confined to one 9.4 mm strip in V1/V2 (all five on the north edge),
so the enclosure can be stepped; the RB-02 layout had them on the south edge in the same way. Side-entry electrode connectors
(mated height 5.5 mm) would lower the strip to about 9.5 mm but change footprints/BOM: a separate decision, not proposed here.

### 1.5 Protection from compression and serviceability

* Keep the cell clear of the post: the BAT+ post is trimmed ≈ 1.0 mm below the board and ends in a Ø3.0 exposed VBAT pad; both
  pockets keep ≥ 12 mm away. Use a foam/adhesive layer and an insulating sheet between B.Cu and the cell.
* The cell must not carry the PCB: support the PCB on enclosure standoffs and let the cell float in a foam bed. The tallest
  parts (JST 8.6 mm, module 4.5 mm) are on the top side and cannot press on the cell through the PCB.
* Serviceability options (none validated): **(A)** battery pocket in the underside with its own lid and an insulating partition
  to the PCB; **(B)** fixed under the PCB, enclosure opened from the top side; **(C)** battery in a strap pod connected through J1
  with a longer lead (uses the frozen connector; needs a lead longer than the documented 50–70 mm and strain relief at both ends).
  V1's east-edge plug is reachable from a side hatch; V2's north-edge plug is shared with the cable exit.

Curvature scenarios (flat rigid board across a limb of radius R) are parametric only: edge lift = R − √(R² − (c/2)²) for the

across width cannot exceed 2R without overhanging the limb.

## 4. Is 18 mm versus 16 mm a meaningful risk?

No. The two values are the nearest hi-Z pad to the module *body*; they differ by 2 mm, well inside any placement
tolerance, and neither is derived from RF behaviour. What decides coupling is not a distance between the module body and the
amplifier: the *antenna element* position (enclosure), supply/ground conduction, and the SPI/clock nets. The frozen design
already carries the mitigations: a solid In1 plane under both variants, 2 mm / 1 mm SPI-to-hi-Z/buffer clearance rules, an RC
chain on every input (10 k + 100 pF = 159 kHz corner, then 1 k + 1 nF), and TLV906x amplifiers whose datasheet lists
EMIRR IN+ = 78 dB at 2.4 GHz (<https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9064.pdf>, as retrieved; verify at BOM freeze). The current
RB-02 board already had 30.6 mm; the real change is 31 → 16–18 mm, which is a reason to **verify at bring-up** (noise with BLE
transmitting, antenna in the intended position) rather than a reason to reject either variant. No RF performance is inferred.

## 5. Selection

| Criterion | V1 | V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Area / across width | 3204 mm² / 51.3 mm | 3558 mm² (+11 %) / 60.4 mm |
| Battery fit (documented classes) | 503035, 503040 | 503035, 503040 |
| Lead / power path | J1 close to the post (18.6 mm) | J1 on the cable edge, 40.1 mm from the post |
| Routing room | adequate (9 crossing nets, B.Cu free) | easier (5.7 mm minimum channel) |
| Cable edge | cables north, battery plug east | cables and battery plug both north (crowded edge) |
| Economic edge rules | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |
| Enclosure thickness | same | same |

V2's benefits (wider channels, a corner for lugs) do not remove a constraint V1 has; V1 has the shorter across width and a
shorter power path. **V1 is feasible without important compromises, so V1 is selected.** V2 stays as the fallback if bring-up or
routing shows the north-west of V1 too tight.

## 6. Remaining uncertainties

1. Battery model, thickness, lead length and PCM size (only documented envelopes used); the 10 mm PCM zone is an assumption.
2. Enclosure wall thickness, wire-bend allowance and module height are assumed values.
3. U.FL antenna element placement and the λ/10 keep-away are heuristics; no RF measurement exists.
4. No body measurements; strap type, lug design and comfort are unvalidated until a prototype exists.
5. Hi-Z-to-module distance 31 → 18 mm needs a bring-up noise check.
6. JLC acceptance (tab positions, extended parts) is unchanged and unproven; edge rules are met by construction.
7. Skin-side pads need an enclosure/partition decision.
8. RB-03 must also decide corner radius (≤ 3.7 mm available), mounting holes (new non-electrical footprints), and whether to keep
   top-entry electrode connectors.
