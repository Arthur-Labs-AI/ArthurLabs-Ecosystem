Principles

Arthur is a quiet place to think. The interface is the page, not a frame around the page.

  • Colour means a relation or a state, never a category. Idea kinds are told apart by shape. Accent appears only for “current”, focus and selection.
  • Solid is yours, dashed is Arthur's. Anything the model proposed carries a hollow dashed dot until it is something you wrote or accepted.
  • Every thought has a margin. Source, connections and revisions sit in the same place on every row, inspector and node.
  • Structure is hairlines and whitespace. Only the composer, popovers and the legend are raised. No card inside a card, ever.
  • Two graphs, two grammars. Connections is organic and spatial. Evolution is rectilinear and temporal. They never share node shapes.

Typography

Newsreader carries anything the user thinks or Arthur distils: titles, bodies, quotes. Hanken Grotesk carries anything the interface says. The split itself is the cue: serif is content, sans is chrome.

Typographic rules

  • Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps labels, no letter-spaced eyebrows.
  • Section titles in the inspector are 13px sans medium, not small caps. The hairline above is the divider.
  • Day groups (“Today”, “Yesterday”) are italic serif: they read as notebook headings, not UI chrome.
  • Numerals are tabular wherever they align in columns: counts, timestamps, diff stats.

Colour

Graphite ground with a faint green-grey cast. One accent. Seven relation hues that only reinforce a geometry that already carries the meaning.

Ground and ink

Accent and origin

Relations (colour is secondary to line geometry)

Accent behaviour

  • Where it appears: current revision, selected row marker (2px), focus ring, the Speak button, selected-node ring, provenance highlight underline.
  • Where it never appears: primary buttons (those are ink-on-ground), headings, icons, links, backgrounds, gradients.
  • One accent hue at a time on screen. Only one element per view is filled with it.

Surfaces, borders, radii

Four levels, but only the first is used for content. Everything else is a hairline.

GroundThe page. Rows, inspector and graph canvas all sit directly on it.
SunkenThe rail and inputs. Recedes rather than rises.
RaisedThe composer only. The one surface that invites you in.
OverlayMenus, the legend, tooltips. The only shadows in the product.

Radii: by role, never one value everywhere

xs 3 · chips, kbd, marks
sm 6 · buttons, inputs, rows
md 11 · composer, popovers
full · Speak, status dot only

Borders

  • line-soft between rows and sections. line around raised things and controls. ink-4 on hover.
  • Selection is a 2px accent bar on the leading edge plus a 8% ink wash. Never an outline box.

Spacing and density

4px base. Comfortable rows are 68px with a snippet; compact rows are 48px and drop the snippet. Density is a user setting, and the same tokens drive it.

Icons and kind glyphs

UI icons are 1.5px stroke on a 24 grid (Lucide-compatible, so Lucide can supply the generic ones). The five idea kinds and seven relations are custom: they are Arthur's vocabulary and must not look like a stock icon set.

UI icons

Mark

Two legs meet at a single node. Read one way it is a letter, read another it is a thought that branches. The node is the only accent-coloured part and is the favicon on its own. Wordmark is “Arthur” in Newsreader 500. The expanded “A.R.T.H.U.R.” appears only in About and documentation.

Navigation

A 216px rail. Capture is the one filled control because capture is the one verb you perform from everywhere. Ask Arthur has a reserved slot, disabled until recall exists.

States

  • Rest: ink-2 on sunken
  • Hover: 4.5% ink wash, ink text
  • Current: 8% wash, 2px accent bar sitting on the rail's outer edge
  • Focus: 2px accent ring, 2px offset
  • Unavailable: ink-4 with a “soon” note, not removed

Buttons and inputs

Buttons

  • One primary (ink fill) per view. Capture, or the single decisive action.
  • Default is outlined, ghost has no border until hover.
  • Labels are verbs that match the result: “Revise” produces a revision.

Inputs

Filters are underline tabs, not pills. Empty kinds dim, but stay put so the layout never shifts.

Idea rows

The most repeated component in Arthur. The margin cluster (source, connections, revisions, age) is a fixed-width column set so scanning is vertical.

Anatomy

  • Kind glyph (shape) · serif title · one-line muted snippet (comfortable only) · margin: source, connection count, revision number, age.
  • Selected = 2px accent bar + wash. Hover = wash only. No border box, no shadow.

Inspector

A page, not a card. 430px on Ideas, 392px on Connections. The same sections in the same order everywhere: content → Source → Connections → Revisions. Each section header links to its dedicated screen.

Connections: node and edge language

Shape says what an idea is. Line geometry says how two ideas relate. Colour is a reinforcement for people who can use it, never the only signal.

Nodes by kind

Node states

Edges

Rules

  • Node size grows with number of connections (12–26px), so hubs read at a glance.
  • Edge labels appear only for edges in the current focus. An unselected graph has zero edge text.
  • Selecting a node dims everything outside its neighbourhood to 34%. Selecting an edge dims everything but its two ends.
  • Region names (“Capture & cognition”) are quiet italic serif, like names on an old map. They are not boxes.
  • Origin: dashed hollow dot = proposed by Arthur; solid dot = created by you or the system.

Evolution: revision language

A rail, not a network. Time runs left to right. Revisions are rounded rectangles (never circles) joined by right-angle rails with rounded corners. Two lanes: the line of thought, and branches.

Origins

  • First capture: solid outline
  • Your edit: solid outline
  • From a new capture: pencil-blue outline (Arthur-derived)
  • Merge: underlined box, two rails in

States

  • Current: accent fill. Exactly one.
  • Selected: ink outline + faint halo
  • Parent of selected: its rail turns accent

Diff

  • Word-level, deterministic, inline.
  • Insert in green wash, delete struck in coral. Never colour alone: delete is also struck through.

Empty, loading, error

Empty states are an instruction. Errors say what happened and what to do, without apologising. Loading preserves layout.

Empty: Ideas

Nothing captured yet

Say or type a thought. Arthur turns it into ideas and keeps the original words next to them.

Loading: Connections

Finding connections. Checking each proposal twice.

Error: Evolution
Couldn't save the merge.
R2 and R3 are unchanged. Check disk space, then try again.

Motion

Motion answers an action. Nothing animates on its own except one moment: when you capture, a thread draws from the new capture to the ideas distilled from it.

  • 120ms state change (hover, press). 220ms structural (focus-dim in graph, inspector swap). Curve: cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1).
  • The thread: 600ms line draw from the capture node down to each new idea, staggered 80ms. Plays once.
  • Evolution: a new revision's rail draws left to right (240ms) and its box fades in at the end.
  • Graph: never auto-settles or floats. Layout is computed once and stable, so the map is the same map tomorrow.
  • Reduced motion: every transition and animation becomes instant, the thread appears fully drawn.

Don'ts

  • No cards inside cards, no equal-height tile grids.
  • No stat tiles: counts live inside the thing they count.
  • No gradient, glow, glass or blur. The only shadow is on overlays.
  • No sparkle icon as decoration. Arthur's presence is its provenance and proposals, not an emoji.
  • No colour per idea kind.
  • No edge text in an unselected graph.
  • No circles in Evolution, no rectangles for ideas in Connections.
  • No metrics we cannot compute from the data model.
