Time
R3 of R5

The bet

The graph is the home screen. Every other view is a lens on the same map. Time is a scrubber, not a screen.

Strengths

  • Most memorable demo; the competition screenshot
  • Connections and Evolution feel native, not bolted on
  • Honest to the “connected thinking” promise

Risks

  • Collapses at scale. 12 nodes are lovely; 400 are hairballs.
  • Text-heavy work (tasks, notes) is awkward on a canvas
  • It lands in the neon-HUD territory we were asked to avoid: glow, blur, glass
  • Hardest to adapt to mobile; needs a different app

The bet

A thought is a page with a margin. Source, connections and revisions are annotations in that margin, everywhere. The graph and the lineage are what you get when you zoom out.

Strengths

  • Makes the three concepts part of every thought, as asked
  • Reads as a notebook, not a dashboard; serif is the cue that it is your content
  • Scales: rows, hairlines and one inspector survive 5,000 ideas
  • Mobile: margin collapses under the thought, inspector becomes a sheet

Risks

  • Quieter than Atlas in a 30-second demo, so Connections must carry the wow
  • Serif in a dark app is uncommon; must be kept disciplined
  • Needs custom glyphs and relation art, not stock Lucide
